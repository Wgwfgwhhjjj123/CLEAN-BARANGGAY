How it works

From report to clean street in four steps

  1. 1

    Snap a photo

    Take a "before" photo of the waste and describe where it is.

  2. 2

    Submit your report

    Your barangay admin receives it right away.

  3. 3

    Admin cleans up

    After the cleanup, the admin uploads an "after" photo.

  4. 4

    Rate the result

    Compare before and after, then leave a rating and feedback.

About BasuReport

Working Together for a Cleaner Future.

BasuReport is a community-focused waste reporting platform designed to make reporting garbage concerns easier and more accessible. It helps residents participate in keeping their surroundings clean and encourages collective responsibility for a healthier environment.
