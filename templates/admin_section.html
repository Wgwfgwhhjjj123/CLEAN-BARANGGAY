{% elif section == 'benefits' %}

Expected Benefits

How BasuReport supports residents, barangay staff, and a cleaner community.

♟

Simple community reporting

Residents can submit waste concerns with a location, description, and photo, making reports easier to understand and review.

▥

More organized follow-up

Report statuses help administrators see which issues are pending, accepted, being addressed, completed, or rejected.

◷

Clearer communication

Notifications and announcements can keep residents informed as reports are reviewed and community cleanup activities are shared.

♻

Cleaner shared spaces

Making concerns visible can encourage timely action and participation in keeping neighborhood spaces clean and healthy.

These are intended benefits; actual results depend on community participation and timely report follow-up.

{% elif section == 'methodology' %}

Development Methodology

An iterative, user-focused approach for building and improving BasuReport.

  1. 1

    Understand the need

    Identify the reporting and cleanup coordination needs of residents and barangay administrators.

  2. 2

    Plan and design

    Organize requirements into clear user flows, page layouts, and report-status steps before implementation.

  3. 3

    Build in small increments

    Develop related features such as accounts, reports, notifications, and dashboard tools in manageable pieces.

  4. 4

    Test the workflows

    Check page navigation, form validation, access controls, and report state changes, including common error cases.

  5. 5

    Review and improve

    Use testing and user feedback to refine usability, accessibility, and reliability in the next iteration.

This page describes the project’s iterative development approach; it does not represent a formal certification or audit.
